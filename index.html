Syllabus: Edexcel 4ET1 • Of Mice and Men

In dramatizing the predatory vulnerability of migrant labor, Steinbeck depicts Lennie's physical presence through "bear-like animalistic kinetic verbs" , instantly establishing an instinctual innocence unsuited to a harsh, Darwinian economic system.

By beginning and concluding the novella by the verdant pool of the Salinas River: "live off the fatta the lan'."

Steinbeck constructs an inescapable circular narrative frame, underscoring that for dispossessed workers during the Great Depression, the pastoral dream was fated to end in tragedy.
